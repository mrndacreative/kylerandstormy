Our Story

It all started with a swipe on Hinge. At the time, Stormy was living in College Station while Kyler was about an hour and a half away. That distance didn't last long, though. On our first date, we spent the evening axe throwing, where Kyler was determined to impress so determined, in fact, that he secretly recruited a friend to practice the week before. What started as a one date turned into many more.

Not long after, Kyler took Stormy skydiving where Kyler jumped first, landing ahead of Stormy, and there waiting below was a banner asking her to be his girlfriend. It was one of those moments we'll always remember, and a pretty accurate preview of the adventures we'd continue to share together.

As the years passed, we built a home together and grew our little family. Zara came first, followed by Zuko, who quickly became the perfect addition to our pack. Since then, we've been fortunate to experience so much together, from traveling to new countries and chasing unforgettable experiences to simply enjoying quiet nights at home.

Our travels led us to the breathtaking mountains of St. Moritz, Switzerland, where Kyler planned the most unforgettable surprise: high above the Alps, on a secluded helicopter landing pad, he got down on one knee and asked Stormy to spend forever with him. Moments later, we boarded a helicopter and flew even deeper into the mountains for an intimate private dinner, where we celebrated the beginning of our next chapter.

Travel has become one of the things we love most as a couple, and few places have left a bigger impression on us than the French Riviera. From the first time we visited, we knew it was somewhere we wanted to return to again and again. That's why choosing Monaco for our wedding felt so natural.

We're incredibly grateful that you're making the journey to celebrate with us. Being surrounded by our favorite people, in one of our favorite places in the world, doing some of our favorite things is everything we could have hoped for. We can't wait to share this unforgettable weekend with you!

F. A. Q.’s

When should I arrive?

No later than August 26 if you plan to attend the welcome dinner and other activities!

What is the nearest airport?

Nice Côte d'Azur Airport (NCE), 30 minutes from Monaco.

How should I get around?

We do not recommend renting a car. The streets are super tight and difficult to drive in. Ubers are very prevalent and reasonably priced, and taxis are everywhere. Note: it WILL be hard to find transportation from Monaco to France as Ubers are prohibited from picking up in Monaco. Our venue is on the French side of the border, so no need to worry there!

Do I need any special documents?

Ensure your passport expiration date is April 2028 or later. No EES (visa for Americans traveling to the EU) required at this time, but subject to change.

Are children invited?

Our wedding weekend is an adults-only celebration. We truly appreciate your understanding and can't wait to celebrate with you!

How much should I budget for?

The French Riviera is comparable in price to major U.S. cities such as New York, Los Angeles, or Dallas. While it's certainly possible to splurge, there are plenty of excellent restaurants and activities at reasonable prices. You don't need to spend extravagantly to have a great time!

As a reference, an Uber from the airport to most hotels typically costs around $65, and you can enjoy a quality waterfront dinner for around $40 per person before drinks.

We recommend budgeting $1,000 per person for solo travelers, or $750 per person when traveling as a couple or group. This should comfortably cover your hotel, transportation, meals, drinks, and activities for the wedding weekend, with the final cost depending on your preferred hotel and dining style.

Getting There

The closest airport is Nice Côte d'Azur Airport (NCE), located 30 minutes from Monaco.

There are 6 nonstop routes to NCE operated by US-based-carriers, operating from ATL, BOS, JFK, EWR, PHL, and IAD. If your home airport does not offer nonstop service, we recommend connecting through one of these gateways or via a major European hub such as LHR, CDG, AMS, FRA, or ZRH.
